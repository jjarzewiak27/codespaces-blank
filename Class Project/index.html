This site is here to help you recognize phishing emails, phone scams, Fake Logins, Gift Card Scams and other tricks criminals use to steal money and personal information.

Education

Deception is what Hackers/Scammers use to gain your trust or trick you. They use techniques like Phishing, Deception, Vishing, and other more advanced techniques to gain access to your personal information.

How do these hackers gain your trust? Well there are some common ways to gain information about you or your loved ones through the internet. They can use prompting through emails to lure you into giving secure information. All of these techniques hackers use are designed to Create a believeable story which includes deceptive content in order to gain your trust. Once they have your trust then they can complete their ultimate goal of impersonating you or stealing information.

How can you prevent Scammers or Hackers?

  • Always question any links that you might be about to click a link on.
  • Make sure you verify numbers or emails before answering.
  • Never give out personal information unless you are 100% sure of who you are talking to.
  • Use strong passwords and change them often.
  • Use multi-factor authentication when available.
