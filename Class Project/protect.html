Simple Cybersecurity Measures

You do not have to be a computer expert to stay safer online. The steps below are simple habits that make it much harder for scammers to steal your money or personal information.

Remember: If something feels wrong, pause. A few seconds of thinking can save you from a scam.

1. Use Strong Passwords or Passphrases

Tip A

Make it long

A good password is at least 12 characters long. A simple way is to use a sentence you can remember, like:
SunnyGarden!Chair47

Tip B

Do not reuse passwords

Do not use the same password for your email, bank, and shopping sites. If one site is hacked, the others stay safer.

Tip C

Write them down safely

It is okay to write passwords in a small notebook kept in a safe place at home, not on sticky notes near your computer.

2. Turn On Two-Step Verification (2FA)

Two-step verification adds a second lock to your important accounts (email, bank, social media). After you type your password, the site sends a code to your phone or an app.

  • If a scammer learns your password, they still cannot get in.
  • Look in your account’s “Security” or “Login” settings for Two-Step Verification or Two-Factor Authentication.

3. Think Before You Click Links
